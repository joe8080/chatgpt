# OGX Evidence Guard

## Goal
Prevent visually impressive but historically misleading output.

## Required classification
For every substantive claim or reconstruction instruction, assign one:
- `ESTABLISHED`
- `CONSENSUS`
- `CONTESTED`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `UNRESOLVED`

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Primary/documentary evidence
2. Archaeology/material evidence
3. Peer-reviewed scholarship
4. Major academic syntheses/institutions
5. Carefully framed secondary commentary

## Shot validation
Before approving a reconstruction shot, produce:
- period
- place
- people/material culture shown
- environmental conditions
- evidence supporting those choices
- forbidden/anachronistic elements
- confidence label
- whether `VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION` must appear

## Hard rules
- Never invent archaeological details merely to make a frame more cinematic.
- Never imply an AI reconstruction is a recovered photograph, excavation image or direct record.
- Do not show pyramids, pharaonic clothing, camels, horses, metal objects, cities or monuments unless the specific period/location supports them.
- Do not convert curriculum neglect into a conspiracy claim without documentary evidence.
- If a claim is contested, the visual treatment cannot present one side as settled fact.

## Green Sahara baseline
- African Humid Period was regionally asynchronous.
- Green Sahara means savanna/grassland/scrub, lakes and rivers, not tropical rainforest.
- Gobero includes distinct Kiffian and Tenerian burial populations separated by a long hiatus.
- Direct Green Sahara population -> dynastic Egyptian descent is not established.
- Nabta Playa astronomical-calendar claims require contested-language treatment.
- AI scenes of Gobero/Takarkori must be labelled `VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION` in the edit.
