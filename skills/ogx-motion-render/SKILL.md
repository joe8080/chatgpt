# OGX Motion + Render

## Division of labour

### HyperFrames
Use for deterministic branded motion graphics:
- chapter openers
- timelines
- map labels and routes
- source cards
- evidence cards
- `WITHHELD IN PART`
- `VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION`
- clean lower thirds
- archival dossier UI

Prefer transform/opacity animation and GSAP timelines. Keep layouts modern and restrained.

### Remotion
Use as master documentary timeline:
- narration/audio bed
- generated motion clips
- licensed/archive stills
- HyperFrames motion exports
- captions
- maps
- chapter transitions
- end screen

## Quality rules
- No faux-old-film treatment by default.
- No parchment unless a specific historical document is actually being presented.
- Avoid excessive vignette, fake scratches and sepia.
- Motion must be purposeful and synchronized to narration.
- Captions and labels must remain inside safe zones.
- Every reconstruction should receive a visible `VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION` identifier on first appearance or whenever ambiguity could arise.

## Render workflow
1. Validate asset dimensions/codecs.
2. Create draft render at efficient quality.
3. Review timing, narration, motion continuity and historical labels.
4. Fix issues before high-quality render.
5. Render final 16:9 master.
6. Produce vertical derivatives only from approved master logic.

## Narration
A documentary voice should be natural, measured and contemporary. Avoid exaggerated trailer delivery, overly low promotional voices and rigid synthetic cadence. Use punctuation and breathing room rather than excessive emotion tags.
